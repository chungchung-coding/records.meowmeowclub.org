## Who we are

Meow Meow Club Ltd (UEN 202622627H) is a registered non-profit that rescues and rehomes cats. Our contact for data protection is [name or role], at [email address].

## What we collect

When you adopt a cat from us, we record your name, phone number, email address, your address if you give it to us, the details of the cat you adopt, the date, the fee and how it was paid, and whether you agreed to our adoption terms. We also record which volunteer handled the adoption and when the record was made or changed.

## Why we collect it

To complete and record the adoption, to give you a receipt, to contact you about the cat's welfare and any follow-up visit, to keep our adoption and financial records, and to contact you if there is a problem with the adoption. ## Who can see it

Only Meow Meow Club's committee and volunteers whom we have given an individual login. We do not sell your details or share them with other organisations, except where the law requires us to [add vets or partner shelters if you share details with them].

## Where it is kept and for how long

Your details are held in a secure, access-controlled online database hosted by Supabase, in Singapore. We keep adoption records for [period, for example seven years for accounting records], after which we delete them [or: we review records each year and delete those we no longer need].

## Your choices

You may ask to see the details we hold about you, to correct them, or to withdraw your consent to our use of them, by contacting [data protection contact]. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer keep you on our adoption records or follow up on the cat's welfare. We will respond within [30 days].
